# Product Brief

## Product Name

Corporate Learning Progress, Intervention & Compliance Tracking System

## Product Vision

Enable organizations to proactively track employee learning progress, identify learners at risk of missing required competencies, trigger timely interventions, and produce compliance-ready evidence for internal and external stakeholders.

## Problem Summary

Organizations are accountable for training attendance, learning outcomes, competency completion, and regulatory compliance. Today, learning data is fragmented across LMS platforms, assessment tools, spreadsheets, and trainer notes. This fragmentation causes late detection of employees who are falling behind, making interventions reactive instead of proactive.

## Product Goals

- Consolidate learning attendance, assessment, and competency milestone data into a single learner profile.
- Identify at-risk learners early using configurable risk rules.
- Track intervention actions such as remedial training, coaching, and mentoring.
- Measure intervention outcomes and learner improvement.
- Provide dashboards and reports for trainers, L&D administrators, managers, and compliance stakeholders.

## Non-Goals

- The product will not replace a full LMS.
- The product will not deliver course content, host e-learning modules, or manage content authoring.
- The product will not provide advanced HR performance management.
- The product will not automate regulatory decisions without human review.

## Primary Users

- L&D Administrator
- Trainer
- Manager
- Compliance Officer
- Employee Learner
- System Administrator

## Product Scope

In scope:

- API-based ingestion of attendance records.
- API-based ingestion of assessment scores.
- Ingestion and tracking of competency-level milestones.
- Rule-based risk identification.
- At-risk learner classification.
- Intervention planning and tracking.
- Compliance-ready reports.
- Simple dashboards for operational visibility.

Out of scope:

- Course content creation.
- Content delivery player.
- Payment, certification marketplace, or external course catalog.
- Full employee HR lifecycle management.

## Key Success Measures

- Percentage of required learning records successfully ingested.
- Percentage of at-risk learners detected before final compliance deadline.
- Reduction in delayed interventions.
- Percentage of interventions with recorded outcomes.
- Accuracy and usefulness of risk rules for trainers and L&D administrators.
- Compliance report completeness and audit readiness.
